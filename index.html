Heart & Grain

Coffee and pastry shop, Angeles

A homemade Kapampangan coffee and bread shop. The identity pairs a hand-lettered script with a forest green and cream palette, then carries it onto signage, cups and coffee bags.

Pugon Café

Filipino café identity

A modern take on the warmth of Filipino coffee culture. The palette comes from a traditional pugon: earthy, comforting and familiar. The system covers a primary and secondary mark, social posts, cups, bread boxes, pastry bags, a storefront and signage.

Tindahan ni Kokoy

Candy shop, San Fernando, Pampanga

A little candy shop from your childhood, redesigned for today. Merienda and pasalubong in a striped-candy mark, shown here in a green and a brown colorway with a brand sheet.

ShenShop

Social ads for a shop-management app

Two ad layouts for an app that handles sales, purchases, inventory and repairs in one place: a light version built around the four features, and a dark, high-contrast version with store download buttons.

WOW Solar

Solar company, Lubao, Philippines

Two logo systems for a solar company: the main WOW Solar mark and a WOW Solar PH wordmark, each in light and dark versions. They come with Facebook cover designs that pair the logo with contact details and a projects strip.
